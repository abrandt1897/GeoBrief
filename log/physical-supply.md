## East-West line after the Khurais strike (Oct 5, late) — status CONTESTED

**Bottom line:** leans **flowing, possibly at a reduced rate, after a brief weekend halt** (~75%). ~25% it is effectively down for days (would fire "East-West/Yanbu offline for days" → infra ~16%). Not confirmed: no tracking data and no Saudi statement since the strike.

**Confirmed (high confidence):**

- A real, large strike/fire at **Pumping Station No. 2 (Khurais, east of Riyadh)** on Oct 4: Sentinel-3 shows a ~50 km black smoke plume; NASA FIRMS logged 4 heat anomalies, FRP up to 88 MW, one pixel at the VIIRS 367 K ceiling (HormuzLetter / OSINT, via No1's Daily Digest). Houthis claimed it (Saree). Damage is not in doubt; "restored" ≠ "unharmed."
- The line **was halted at least briefly** over the weekend — AFP's source says so and Bloomberg acknowledges a brief weekend halt.
- **Pre-strike throughput was high:** Bloomberg Oct 2 (one unnamed person) — ~6 Mbd, >80% of 7 Mbd nameplate, ~4.5 Mbd left for export after west-coast refineries. The Kpler ~2.65 Mbd figure (≈Sep 30) is superseded.
- Saudi government and Aramco have said **nothing**.

**Why it leans "flowing":**

- Two independent wires vs one: **Bloomberg** ("people familiar") and **Reuters** (unnamed source, per CBS/Yahoo) say oil is flowing Monday; **AFP** (one unnamed Saudi energy-sector source) says "big damage… pipeline stopped again."
- **Market vote:** Brent rose up to 1.2% on the AFP report and pared the gain on Bloomberg's.
- **Aramco cut Nov Arab Light OSP to Asia** $3 to Oman/Dubai −$5, a six-year low (Reuters, Oct 5) — a seller bracing for a supply outage doesn't usually cut price to win volume. Weak signal: the OSP may have been set regardless of Khurais.
- Plausible reconciliation (inference, not reported): the system can likely run derated around one damaged pumping station, so AFP may describe the weekend halt and Bloomberg/Reuters Monday's restart.

**Why it is NOT confirmed:**

- Every flow claim on both sides is a single anonymous source; Yeni Şafak / Anadolu / Core Insights are rewrites, not independent confirmation.
- No Kpler / Vortexa / TankerTrackers reading on East-West throughput or Yanbu loadings after Oct 4.
- Throughput rate after restart is unknown — "flowing" could be well below the pre-strike ~6 Mbd.

**What resolves it:** Yanbu/Al Muajjiz loadings for Oct 4–8 (Kpler, Vortexa, TankerTrackers satellite counts), or an Aramco/Energy Ministry statement. Check on the next runs.

**Arab Light OSP (context):** Arab Light = Saudi flagship medium-sour grade (~33° API, ~2% S, mostly Ghawar). OSP = Aramco's monthly differential to a benchmark (Asia: Oman/Dubai average). Reuters rationale for the Nov cut: record freight (VLCC Gulf→China >$30/bbl, FOB pricing so buyer pays), market share after war disruptions, and delays/longer voyages for cargoes loaded at Sidi Kerir (Egypt, via SUMED). A discount to a war-elevated benchmark, not cheap oil. Minor for US pump prices.

**Uhud (3rd UAE-linked hit, Oct 2):** ~46k DWT products tanker, built 2003, Panama flag; registered owner Master Energy LLC, commercial/ISM manager Emirates Shipping LLC (UAE) (MagicPort). Regional product trader (Fujairah, Hamriya, Khor Fakkan, Sohar, Port Sudan) — **not** a Saudi shuttle or STS-loop VLCC, so tw_saudi_ship does not fire.

**Oct 6 update:** Iran International reports the East-West line delivered 5.8M bbl to Yanbu by Tuesday morning — a cumulative/single-source figure, not a rate; supports "flowing" but still no Kpler/Vortexa Yanbu loadings. Houthis claim a strike on the Rabigh refinery (fed by an East-West spur); unconfirmed.
